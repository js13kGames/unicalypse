UNICALYPSE

Storm the evil unicorn mothership. The rainbow leads to the portal. When it sets, the portal closes - make it through in time.

THE GAME

You are aboard a unicorn mothership, seven decks deep. Each deck is a freshly generated maze; somewhere in it a portal leads one deck further in. A rainbow hangs in the sky over the portal's direction and sinks lower as your time runs out. When it touches the horizon, you are out of time.

Seven decks, seven colours of the spectrum — from a calm violet start to a red finale. Clear all seven and Earth survives the night.

----

Aim with the mouse, move with the keyboard. Be like John Wick, but with rainbows and unicorns.
